# Orla Browser — Website Design Contract

## Purpose
Persuade visitors to understand the Windows browser preview, experience its real feature categories, and reach the legitimate download path.

## Core creative idea
Continuity of context. Each workspace represents a different thread of work. The browser interface is the hero, not abstract decorative cards.

## Visual language
- Dark mineral foundation (#080d0c) and warm off-white product typography (#f2f4ec)
- Citron-green accent (#d8fb82), reserved for selection and primary calls to action
- Light demonstration chapter (#e9ece2), green brand chapter (#dbe8ba)
- Existing self-hosted Manrope, concise copy and considered line breaks
- Maximum editorial display near 96px; no text gradients, fabricated performance numbers or fake endorsements
- Original contour geometry and a detailed simulated browser UI with disclosure of illustrative data
- No generic glass cards, repetitive icon grids or gratuitous decorative gradients

## Signature interaction
The Orla interface arrives in perspective and settles with scrolling. Visitors can switch Workspaces, Split view and Captures, with interruptible transitions and honest UI feedback. The coastline SVG draws a relationship between independent project threads.

## Motion and quality
- One authored focal sequence, quieter supporting section transitions
- Reduced motion alternative; no autoplay audio or scroll-jacking
- Pause WebGL rendering out of view; cap canvas DPR; keep static fallback
- Focus/keyboard support, responsive layouts at 390, 768 and 1440px
- Confirm build and visual screenshots separately; a successful Vercel build does not prove visual QA

## Reference interpretation
Raycast: software-first. TasteCode: product demonstration. Taste Skill: intentional composition. ArtCraft: strong layout rhythm and bespoke vector geometry. Do not reproduce these sites.
